**Palmera Bay: Sunset Heat** is an open-world crime game that runs in a desktop browser from its own files (three.js, no network). Play as Marisol "Mari" Vega, home on a sun-soaked island city to get her brother out of jail and take down the Gold Tide syndicate.

- **A whole island city** — nine districts (Downtown Solmar's glass towers, art-deco hotels on Ocean Drive, Neon Mile clubs, Mangrove Docks, suburbs, parks) plus a long beach with a pier, all generated from one seed.
- **On foot and by car** — walk, sprint, jump, punch and shoot; carjack any of 8 original vehicles (Vespera GT, Caiman SUV, Coastline Cab, Bay PD Cruiser, Bulwark armored van...) with arcade drift physics, damage, smoke and explosions.
- **A living street** — AI traffic follows right-hand lanes and turns at junctions; pedestrians walk the sidewalks, stroll the beach and flee from gunfire.
- **Wanted level 1–5** — Bay PD cruisers route through the grid to you, officers bail out to arrest or shoot, a helicopter joins at 4 stars; break line of sight to cool off or respray at Fresh Coat Garage. WASTED / BUSTED with hospital and bail fees.
- **Story + side content** — 5 story missions (delivery against the clock, collections, a beach shootout, the armored-van heist), a street race, taxi fares, 12 hidden seashells, a gun shop with unlocks, a safehouse that saves.
- **HUD** — rotating radar minimap with blips and cop dots, full island map (M), wanted stars, health/armour, weapon and ammo, speedometer, objectives, dialogue subtitles, synthesized sound and a car radio.

Controls: WASD move/drive · mouse look · click shoot · right-click aim · F enter/exit/carjack · Space jump/handbrake · Shift sprint · Q/E or 1–4 weapons · V camera · H horn · M map · Esc pause.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts    # then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome
```
